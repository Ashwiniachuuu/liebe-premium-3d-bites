# Liebe: Premium 3D Bites

Create a single-page premium futuristic 3D food website called “LIEBE”, inspired by the provided reference image. Do NOT create a generic 2D restaurant website.

Design

Premium white / off-white background

Clean luxury white theme

Premium glassmorphism with translucent white glass panels

Subtle warm beige / soft golden accents

Realistic high-quality burger and food visuals

3D depth, reflections, soft shadows and cinematic lighting

Apple-style premium minimal design

Smooth scroll, parallax and subtle 3D animations

High contrast text using black / dark charcoal

Bright, elegant and sophisticated food-brand aesthetic

Navbar

LIEBE logo with:

Home | Menu | Offers | About | Contact

Use a floating transparent glass navbar with a premium white glass effect.

Hero

Create a fullscreen immersive 3D hero featuring a realistic juicy chicken burger with steam, particles and soft cinematic lighting.

Text:

“Good Food. Good Mood!”

Make “Mood!” a subtle warm golden/beige accent.

Subtitle:

“Fresh flavors. Bold cravings. Delivered your way.”

Buttons:

ORDER NOW | EXPLORE MENU

Use premium dark text with subtle golden/beige accent styling.

Add subtle mouse movement and scroll-based burger animation.

Menu Section

Show premium realistic 3D food cards for:

Cheese Burger

Spicy Chicken Burger

Double Beef Burger

Crispy Chicken

Loaded Fries

Drinks

Include image, name, short description, rating and price.

Use elegant white glassmorphism cards with soft shadows, subtle borders and hover/tilt effects.

Offers Section

Create a cinematic featured offer:

“Spicy Burger Combo – 20% OFF”

Show burger, fries and drink with floating 3D presentation.

Use a bright white premium background with subtle beige/golden ambient lighting and realistic shadows.

About Section

Dark-to-light or premium bright cinematic restaurant/kitchen scene with warm lighting and subtle smoke.

Heading:

“Made Fresh. Made With Passion.”

Add a short premium description about LIEBE.

Keep the section visually consistent with the white luxury theme.

Contact Section

Simple premium white glassmorphism contact section with:

Location | Phone | Email | Opening Hours

Use clean dark typography, subtle borders, soft shadows and minimal luxury styling.

Final CTA

“Ready for Your Next Craving?”

“One bite is all it takes.”

Button:

ORDER YOUR BURGER

Technical

Use React + Three.js/React Three Fiber + Framer Motion/GSAP + CSS3.

Keep everything as ONE LANDING PAGE.

Only implement the Home, Menu, Offers, About and Contact sections with navbar anchor scrolling.

Focus heavily on:

Realistic 3D food

Cinematic lighting

White luxury aesthetic

Premium glassmorphism

Smooth animations

Parallax effects

3D depth

Realistic reflections and shadows

High-end food photography

Premium typography

Modern Apple-style visual quality

Avoid unnecessary pages, dashboards, login systems, cart systems, product-detail pages or complex backend functionality.

Keep the implementation lightweight to minimize credit usage.
